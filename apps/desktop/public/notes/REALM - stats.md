# REALM – Stat Meanings

> ## Reflexes
>
> How quickly you react to danger and opportunity. Covers initiative, dodging, interrupting actions, and split-second decisions.
>
> ​

> ## Endurance
>
> Your physical power and stamina. Determines how hard you can hit, how much you can carry, and how long you can keep going.
>
> ​

> ## Acumen
>
> Your mental sharpness and judgment. Affects perception, problem-solving, tactics, memory, and understanding complex situations.
>
> ​

> ## Legerity
>
> Your finesse and control with your hands and body. Covers stealth, crafting, lockpicking, precision attacks, tool use, and delicate physical tasks.
>
> ​

> ## Magnetism
>
> Your force of personality and social pull. Influences persuasion, leadership, intimidation, deception, and how strongly others are drawn to you.
>
> ​
